Detailed Description

Recognizes single-finger tap gestures by listening to touch events from an ITouchInput source. A tap is defined as a touch that begins and ends within a configurable maximum duration and without exceeding a configurable maximum movement distance. Long presses and drags are ignored.

Subscribe to the Tapped event to receive notifications when a qualifying tap is detected.

A tap is recognized when:

  • A single touch contact begins (TouchPhase.Began).
  • The same contact ends without exceeding MaxTapMovementPixels of movement.
  • The total contact duration is less than or equal to MaxTapDurationSeconds.
