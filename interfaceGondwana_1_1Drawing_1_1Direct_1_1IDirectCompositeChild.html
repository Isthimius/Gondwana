Detailed Description

Defines a movable direct drawable that may be owned by a DirectComposite.

The interface provides the coordinate target and visual operations required for recursive composition without requiring DirectComposite to recognize concrete child types.

Container implementations should apply visibility, Z-order, opacity, and fade operations recursively to their descendants.
