Detailed Description

Recognizes swipe gestures by listening to touch events from an ITouchInput source. A swipe is a single-finger gesture that ends with sufficient speed and with a clear primary direction. Short or slow drags do not qualify.

Subscribe to the Swiped event to receive notifications when a qualifying swipe is detected.

A swipe is recognized when a touch contact ends and the average speed over the contact duration meets or exceeds MinimumSwipeSpeedPixelsPerSecond.
