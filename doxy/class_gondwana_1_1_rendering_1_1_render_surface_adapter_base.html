void Gondwana.Rendering.RenderSurfaceAdapterBase.Render ( SKImage bufferImage,
SKRectI bufferRect,
SKRect destRect )
abstract

Renders the specified portion of the Backbuffer image to the destination rectangle on the RenderSurfaceAdapter.

The method maps the specified region of the buffer image to the destination rectangle, scaling or transforming as necessary. Callers must ensure that the dimensions and coordinates of bufferRect and destRect are valid.

Parameters
bufferImageThe source image to render from. Cannot be null.
bufferRectThe rectangular region of the buffer image to render. Coordinates are in the buffer image's space.
destRectThe rectangular region in the destination space where the rendered content will be drawn.
